Reading the chain…

What holders receive. Rewards are paid in GOOGLc (0xb200…f58B7), a tokenized wrapper issued by the launch platform that tracks the price of Alphabet Inc.’s publicly traded GOOGL stock. Its own name() reads “Alphabet Inc.”, which is the wrapper’s chosen label and not a claim that Alphabet issued it. It is not stock, not a share, and confers no ownership, dividend or voting right in Alphabet Inc. Holding $DOOGLER earns that wrapper token — nothing more and nothing less.

It’s Not Just a Meme. It’s Real Google Lore.

Alphabet’s published Code of Conduct really does say it. Under “Dog Policy”, in the document every Alphabet employee works under:

“Google’s affection for our canine friends is an integral facet of our corporate culture. We like cats, but we’re a dog company, so as a general rule we feel cats visiting our offices would be fairly stressed out.”

— Alphabet Code of Conduct, abc.xyz — Alphabet Code of Conduct, Dog Policy

Googlers who bring their dogs to work are called Dooglers. It is Google’s own term, not a coinage of ours — the company has used it in its own careers and culture material for years, and Doogler merchandise has been sold in the Google store.

At the Mountain View campus, the dog park where they gather is the Dooglerplex — a pun on the Googleplex, and a real fixture of the campus rather than a bit of lore invented for a token.
